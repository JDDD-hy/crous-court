## ⚖️ What is CROUS Court?

CROUS Court is a vibe-coded **community ranking for CROUS food**.

Take a photo of what you actually ate, rate the main dish and sides, and let other students cast their verdicts.

### The tiers

|    🐮    |    👑   |    😎    |    🤖   |     💩    |
| :------: | :-----: | :------: | :-----: | :-------: |
| **GOAT** | **Top** | **Good** | **NPC** | **Trash** |

---

## 🧾 What can you do?

* 🏆 **Browse rankings** by CROUS venue
* 📸 **Upload real tray photos**
* 🗳️ **Vote** for the tier a dish deserves
* 🕵️ **Identify mystery dishes** with the community
* ✨ **Ask AI for clues** when nobody knows what is on the plate
* 📍 **Find CROUS venues** nearby or by search
* 🚩 **Report** incorrect or inappropriate content

---

## 🧑‍⚖️ How does the verdict work?

Every user chooses the tier they believe a dish belongs to.

The community verdict is calculated using the **ordered median** of all valid votes.

Each user gets **one verdict per dish**.

Once submitted, a verdict cannot be changed — so choose carefully before the gavel falls.

## 📖 Our Story

CROUS Court started with a very simple idea:

> **“What if we ranked CROUS food from GOAT to Trash?”**

At first, it was just a joke between me and my roommate.

Then we realized it would be much more fun if the ranking belonged to everyone who actually eats there.

So the joke became a website.

CROUS Court is our attempt to turn everyday meals, mystery side dishes, surprisingly good finds, and regrettable choices into a shared record built by students.

It is not a serious food guide.

Just real CROUS food, real opinions, and a little courtroom drama.

---

## 🔐 A few court rules

CROUS Court is an **independent student project** and is not affiliated with or operated by CROUS.

Community ratings are subjective. They are **not**:

* food safety information
* nutritional advice
* ingredient information
* allergen information

When uploading photos, please avoid visible faces, student IDs, names, or other private information.

**AI suggestions are for reference only. Humans make the final call.**
